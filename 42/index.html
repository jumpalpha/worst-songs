WORST SONG EVER #42

Pass the Casket

◀ #41#0 ▶
🎧 – plays👀 – visits4:01

Lyrics

Intro spoken over clinking forks, a cheerful, perfectly healthy mom

Okay, everybody, phones down.
Before dessert, I'd like to discuss my funeral.
...No, I feel great. I ran a half marathon this morning.

Verse 1 bright log drums, bouncy and warm

Sunday night, there's a roast in the middle of the table
Dad is carving it as slowly as a human being is able
Mom pulls out a binder, laminated, color-tabbed
Section one says "Casket," with a sticky note: "Not drab"
Her blood pressure is perfect and her cholesterol is low
She did Pilates twice today, but she says "you never know"

Pre-Chorus

My brother says, "Mom, you're fifty-two"
She says, "Exactly, sweetie, that's why I've got a lot to do"

Chorus huge, stacked vocals, crowd chant

Pass the potatoes, she's picking the casket
Gravy on the guest list, rolls in the basket
Mom's not dying, she's just super organized
She wants a mahogany box with a lot of storage inside
Pass the potatoes! (Pass 'em!) Pick out a hymn
She's never felt better, the outlook is grim
For us! 'Cause we're here till nine
Planning Mom's funeral... one more time

Verse 2

She wants doves at the service, she wants a hundred and two
And a slideshow of her photos, but no pictures from the zoo
She wants Grandpa doing the eulogy, and Grandpa's ninety-one
He said, "Linda, I'll be dead first," she said, "Then practice, hon"
Then she passed out assignments with a seating chart for crying
My sister got "ugly sobbing," I got "quietly denying"

Pre-Chorus

Dad says, "Can we please just eat?" She says, "Fine, but take a note:
Your speech is running long, and you are not wearing that coat"

Chorus huge, stacked vocals, crowd chant

Pass the potatoes, she's picking the casket
Gravy on the guest list, rolls in the basket
Mom's not dying, she's just super organized
She wants a mahogany box with a lot of storage inside
Pass the potatoes! (Pass 'em!) Pick out a hymn
She's never felt better, the outlook is grim
For us! 'Cause we're here till nine
Planning Mom's funeral... one more time

Bridge music drops to a single log-drum heartbeat, spoken, dead serious

Then Mom stood up and said, "We're doing a rehearsal."
She lay down on the kitchen island. Crossed her arms. Closed her eyes.
Uncle Steve played the trumpet. The dog howled.
We all walked past her, one by one.
And she opened one eye and said,
"Brandon. That was a C-minus grieve."

Breakdown massive drop, gang chant

Again! (Again!) Walk slower! Cry louder!
Again! (Again!) Who brought the chowder?
Mahogany! Hydrangeas! A drone show in the sky!
The greatest funeral ever for a lady who won't die!

Final Chorus key change, choir joins

Pass the potatoes, she's picking the casket
Gravy on the guest list, rolls in the basket
Mom's not dying, she just booked the venue
She already taste-tested the funeral menu
Pass the potatoes! (Pass 'em!) Pick out a hymn
She's never felt better, the outlook is grim
For us! 'Cause she just set the date
For twenty eighty-eight

Outro music cuts, back to clinking forks, spoken

Mom: Same time next Sunday. Bring the binder.
Me: This has been Worst Song Ever, number forty-two.
Mom: Brandon. That was a B-minus outro.
