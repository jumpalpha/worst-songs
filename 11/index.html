My Left Sock Has a Lawyer

Lyrics

Intro

Okay.
Before we start,
my attorney has advised me
not to explain this song.

Verse 1

I woke up Tuesday, quarter past eight,
My left sock served me papers by the garden gate.
Said, “Years of mistreatment, the dryer was cruel,
And pairing me with sandals was against every rule.”

I said, “Sock, be reasonable, we’ve been through a lot.”
He said, “Tell it to my lawyer.”
His lawyer was a POT.

A stainless-steel saucepan in a pinstripe tie,
With a legal pad, a briefcase, and a googly left eye.

Pre-Chorus

The toaster took the witness stand,
The blender yelled, “OBJECTION!”
My pants requested privacy
And entered sock protection.

Chorus

🎵 MY LEFT SOCK HAS A LAWYER!
And apparently I’m going to court!
The charges are confusing,
And the trial is unusually short!

Worst Song Ever #11!
We have officially lost control!
My left sock wants the house,
The car,
And HALF MY OTHER SOCK!

(That doesn't even make sense.)

Verse 2

The judge was just a ceiling fan,
He spun and yelled, “Proceed!”
The jury was twelve chicken wings
Who couldn't even read.

My sock produced Exhibit A:
A photograph of lint.
The courtroom gasped collectively—
I still don't get the hint.

My lawyer was a waffle,
Not licensed in the state.
He whispered, “Plead insanity.”
I whispered, “You're a plate.”

Pre-Chorus

Then somebody played a kazoo,
The courtroom formed a line,
And somehow this became a dance—
At least until nine.

Chorus

🎵 MY LEFT SOCK HAS A LAWYER!
And apparently I’m going to court!
He’s suing for emotional damage
And unauthorized ankle support!

Worst Song Ever #11!
This chorus has nowhere to go!

CLAP! CLAP!

potato.
