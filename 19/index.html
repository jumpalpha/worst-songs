The Other Half of My Sandwich

Lyrics

Verse 1

Love is like a sandwich
That somebody sat upon,
It started out with promise,
Then the good part was all gone.
Love is like a pigeon
When you're eating in the park,
It stares into your eyeballs
Then it follows you till dark.

Chorus

Looooove,
Why you gotta be so love-y?
All the hugging and the kissing
And the calling people "lovey."
Looooove,
You're a weird emotional stew,
I don't know what love is,
But apparently—I love you.

Verse 2

I bought you seventeen roses,
But I left them in the car,
Now they're crispy little flowers
And they smell like old cigars.
I wrote your name in cursive,
Then I spelled your middle wrong,
So I covered it with glitter
And I put it in this song.

Pre-Chorus

They say love is patient,
They say love is kind,
But love stole all the blankets
And kicked me from behind.

Chorus

Looooove,
Why you gotta be so love-y?
You're expensive and confusing
And occasionally cuddly.
Looooove,
You're a heart-shaped barbecue,
I don't know what that means,
But apparently—I love you.

Bridge

If you were a taco,
I'd probably eat you.
If you were a tractor,
I'd probably… seat you?
That rhyme wasn't ready.
I should've stopped at taco.
But love makes people stupid,
So—

TACO!

Key Change Nobody Asked For

LOOOOOOVE!
I can feel it in my spleen!
You're the greatest human person
That my eyeballs ever seen!
LOOOOOOVE!
Like a goose that's learned kung fu,
There are seven billion people—

But I picked...

YOOOOOOOOOOOU!

Love...
