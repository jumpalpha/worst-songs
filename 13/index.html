WORST SONG EVER #13

That Wave Wasn't Mine

◀ #12#14 ▶
🎧 – plays👀 – visits4:27

Lyrics

Intro — hushed, dramatic

I saw a hand rise through the crowded room…
So I raised mine.
That was my first mistake.

Verse 1

I smiled wide and waved hello,
Like we were friends from long ago.
They stared straight through me, cold as stone—
They’d waved at someone else I didn’t know.

I could’ve stopped; I could’ve turned,
Accepted that my pride got burned.
Instead, I waved with both my hands
Like I was guiding planes to land.

Pre-Chorus

One little wave became a scene,
The strangest thing they’d ever seen.
I couldn’t quit—I’d gone too far…
Now everybody thinks I’m in charge!

Chorus

That wave wasn’t mine!
That wave wasn’t mine!
I borrowed it by accident
And crossed a social line.

Now the whole room’s waving,
And nobody knows why—
I started an uprising
With a badly timed “Hi!”

Oh-oh-oh—
That wave wasn’t mine!

Verse 2

A stranger copied what I did,
Then seven grown adults joined in.
Someone shouted, “Pass it on!”
The room erupted before long.

The mayor climbed upon a chair,
Declared a brand-new holiday there.
A marching band came through the door—
Nobody knows who called them for.

Pre-Chorus

The cameras rolled, the banners flew,
A news reporter asked, “Was it you?”
I cleared my throat and took the stand:
“Yes, I’m the founder of the hand.”

Chorus

That wave wasn’t mine!
That wave wasn’t mine!
It somehow crossed three county lines
By half past nine.

Now the whole town’s waving,
There are helicopters in the sky—
I started an uprising
With a badly timed “Hi!”

Oh-oh-oh—
That wave wasn’t mine!

Spoken Interrogation — music drops to finger snaps

“Sir, did you knowingly initiate the gesture?”

I thought they knew me.

“Did they?”

No.

“Then why did you keep waving?”

…Momentum.

Brass Breakdown

Wave to the left!
Wave to the right!
Wave at a stranger
You have never met in your life!

Wave down low!
Wave up high!
Commit to the mistake
And never explain why!

Bridge — suddenly sincere

Maybe every lonely soul
Is waiting to be seen.
Maybe one mistaken wave
Can build a human chain.

Or maybe I embarrassed myself
And made the whole thing weird—
Both interpretations
Are equally revered.

Final Chorus — huge choir

That wave wasn’t mine!
That wave wasn’t mine!
But now it has a logo
And a slogan and a sign.

Every nation’s waving,
Satellites reply—
We finally achieved world peace
With a badly timed “Hi!”

Worst Song Ever #13—

With a badly timed—

Sudden silence

…Oh.

They still weren’t waving at me.
