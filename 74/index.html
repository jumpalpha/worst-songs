Intro log drum hits, shakers, a horse whinny chopped into the beat

Female Vocal Cammie, spoken, deadpan

Okay. Listing goes up... now.
(Neigh!)

Verse 1 Cammie, cool and confident

Took a picture of my horse in the golden-hour light
Typed, "Biscuit, nine years old. Super gentle. Doesn't bite."
Got a studio apartment and the lease says "No horse"
So I listed him for cheap with a heavy heart, of course
First guy knocks at one o'clock, a trailer at the door
Biscuit takes one look at him and starts limping on all four

Pre-Chorus drums drop, just pads and her voice

He was dancing in the paddock, he was racing in the sun
Now he's coughing like a grandpa till the buyer's truck is gone
Then he's fine (He's fine!)
He's totally fine

Chorus full beat, crowd backing vocals

Horse for sale (Or best offer!)
One horse (Or best offer!)
Comes with a saddle and a bag of carrots
Horse for sale (Or best offer!)
Must go, must go, must go
(But Biscuit says no)

Verse 2 Cammie, getting desperate

Second buyer pulls in driving something shiny, something red

Male Vocal big cheerful cowboy buyer

Well, that's a mighty fine horse! I'll pay the price you said!

Female Vocal Cammie

So I'm counting out the twenties and I'm feeling kinda nice
Then Biscuit eats his car keys, and his hat, and his advice
Then he backs into the trailer and he kicks the ramp in half
And the cowboy drives away, and I swear that horse just laughed

Pre-Chorus drums drop, just pads and her voice

Checked my phone at midnight, someone edited the post
Now it says, "Ugly horse. He bites. He smells like burnt toast."
Biscuit, you don't have thumbs! He just looked at me and blinked
And my laptop's warm, and there's hay stuck in the link

Chorus full beat, crowd backing vocals

Horse for sale (Or best offer!)
One horse (Or best offer!)
Comes with a saddle and a bag of carrots
Horse for sale (Or best offer!)
Must go, must go, must go
(But Biscuit says no)

Bridge beat cuts to a lone log drum, Cammie reads slowly, spoken

Then a notification. "New listing near you."
"Cammie. Twenty-three. Some wear and tear.
Good with hay. Cries at movies. Brushes mane okay.
Free to a good home. Must be gone by Friday."
Posted by... Biscuit.
(Neigh.)

Final Chorus biggest beat, choir joins the crowd

Girl for sale (Or best offer!)
One Cammie (Or best offer!)
Comes with a hoodie and a bag of carrots
Girl for sale (Or best offer!)
Must go, must go, must go
(And Biscuit says so)

Outro log drum and shakers thin out, Cammie spoken, deadpan

So now we live together. Nobody got sold.
He's still listed. So am I.
This is Worst Song Ever number seventy-four.
