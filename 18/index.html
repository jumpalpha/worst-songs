WORST SONG EVER #18

Aggressively Beautiful

◀ #17#19 ▶
🎧 – plays👀 – visits4:32

Lyrics

Intro — soft piano, almost whispered

I saw the sunrise hit the lake,
And thought, “Well, this is a mistake.”
The mountains stood there looking fine—
Suspiciously close to the divine.

Verse 1

There was dew upon a spiderweb,
A little bird was making friends,
A butterfly went floating by
Like somebody had rehearsed the sky.

Then suddenly the clouds turned gold,
A perfect breeze began to blow,
And somewhere in a field of wheat
A baby deer stood on its feet.

I said—

Pre-Chorus

“Knock it off.
This is too much.
Nobody needs
This level of stuff.”

Chorus — enormous, emotional

IT'S AGGRESSIVELY BEAUTIFUL!
UNREASONABLY WONDERFUL!
Somebody turn the sunset down,
There's WAY too much magnificence around!

The stars are being irresponsible,
The moon is frankly impossible,
I'M TRYING TO HAVE A NORMAL NIGHT—
BUT EVERYTHING IS BEAUTIFUL TONIGHT!

Verse 2

A grandma held a grandkid's hand,
A dog ran happy through the sand,
Two old people kissed goodbye
And I had to look away and cry.

Then a choir started somewhere near,
Though there was clearly no choir here,
A single leaf fell from a tree
In perfect slow-mo next to me.

I yelled—

Pre-Chorus

“WHO APPROVED THIS?!
WHO SIGNED THE FORM?!
WHY IS THAT HORSE
RUNNING THROUGH THE STORM?!”

Chorus

IT'S AGGRESSIVELY BEAUTIFUL!
BORDERLINE UNCONSTITUTIONAL!
There's sunlight pouring through the rain,
AND NOW A RAINBOW'S DOING IT AGAIN!

The stars are being irresponsible,
The moon remains impossible,
I JUST CAME OUT TO CHECK THE MAIL—
NOW THERE'S A COMET WITH A SPARKLY TAIL!

Instrumental Break

Violins swell dramatically.

Choir enters.

One lonely trumpet.

For absolutely no reason, a children's choir joins.

CHILDREN:
Beautiful...

ADULT CHOIR:
Beautiful...

ONE GUY VERY FAR AWAY:
SO BEAUTIFUUUUUUUL!

Bridge — completely sincere

Maybe beauty isn't quiet.
Maybe sometimes it attacks.
Maybe wonder kicks the door in
And there's simply no going back.

Maybe life keeps leaving little things
We hurry past each day—
Tiny pieces of forever
Hidden in the ordinary way.

Silence

Spoken:

"...dang."

Key Change — unnecessarily enormous

Final Chorus

IT'S AGGRESSIVELY BEAUTIFUL!
CATASTROPHICALLY WONDERFUL!
Every mountain, every tree,
HAS APPARENTLY BEEN WAITING HERE FOR ME!

The whole world's being irresponsible,
Existence is impossible,
AND NOW THE NORTHERN LIGHTS JUST SHOWED—

COME ON, EARTH!

WE GET IT!

YOU'RE PRETTY!

Choir

BEAU-TI-FUUUUUUUUUL!

Outro — tiny piano

This has been
Worst Song Ever #18.

Pause

A squirrel walks past carrying a flower.

Spoken:

"Oh, for crying out loud."
