I'm Sorry, Vending Machine

◀ #15#17 ▶
🎧 – plays👀 – visits5:21

Lyrics

Intro — sparse piano, painfully sincere

I put in two dollars,
I pressed B-4,
The pretzels leaned forward…
Then stopped before the door.

I whispered, “Take your time, friend.”
Like patience might prevail.
Then somebody behind me said,
“Sir… you’re talking to a sale.”

Verse 1

I know it has no feelings,
I know it has no soul,
I know its only purpose
Is exchanging snacks for toll.

But something in that humming
Sounded tired, maybe blue,
So I put my hand against the glass
And said, “I’ve been there too.”

A woman grabbed her daughter,
A janitor walked away,
A security guard radioed,
“We got that guy today.”

But I stood beside that snack machine
Like friendship had begun—
Two lonely souls beneath fluorescent lights,
Except that one was one.

Pre-Chorus

Then the spiral turned…
The pretzels fell…
I gasped like I’d been healed.

And I knew right then
What destiny
Had finally revealed—

CHORUS — enormous arena-rock explosion

🎵 I’M SORRY, VENDING MACHINE!
For all the buttons I pushed too mean!
For every time I shook your frame
And blamed you when the crackers came!

I’M SORRY, VENDING MACHINE!
You’re the bravest rectangle I’ve ever seen!
And if loving you is socially obscene—

Verse 2 — driving guitars

I bought the cheese crackers
Just to boost its self-esteem,
Then purchased seven granola bars
To help it chase its dream.

I didn’t want the raisins,
I don’t even like the gum,
But greatness needs encouragement,
So I bought slot ninety-one.

My bank sent me a warning:
“Unusual spending spree.”
I wrote them back, “It’s personal.
Please let my friend be free.”

Then the manager approached me
With the universal face
Of someone who had absolutely
Had enough of this whole place.

He said, “Sir, you need to leave now.”

I said, “Can Dennis come?”

He said, “Who’s Dennis?”

I pointed.

He went silent.

Then he said…

“…That’s not its name.”

Breakdown — music abruptly stops

What?

Manager, spoken

It’s a SnackMaster 8000.

Singer, devastated whisper

…Dennis lied to me.

Verse 3 — minor-key piano, genuine heartbreak

I stumbled through the parking lot,
The sunset burning red,
Betrayed by one who never spoke
A single word it said.

Was anything between us real?
Did B-4 mean a thing?
Or was I just another fool
Who fell in love with spring?

…Spring-loaded snack dispensers.

Orchestra enters

Then through the automatic doors
I heard a distant sound—

CLUNK.

My final bag of pretzels
Had at last come tumbling down.

I turned.

The machine stood glowing
Like a lighthouse in the rain.

And though it couldn't call my name…

I knew.

Dennis knew.

FINAL CHORUS — full choir, brass, absurdly expensive production

🎵 I’M SORRY, VENDING MACHINE!
You chrome-and-glass carbohydrate queen!
Let the whole food court hear me scream—
You’re more than snacks and caffeine!

I’M SORRY, VENDING MACHINE!
