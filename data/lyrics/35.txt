"One week. One egg. Don't drop it."

Monday mornin', Coach went down the row,
Handed every kid an egg and said, "Don't let it go."
I drew a little smile on him with a ballpoint pen,
Named him Eggward Junior, and I've never been the same again.

[Pre-Chorus]

I keep him in a beanie on the passenger seat...

Best job I've ever had!
