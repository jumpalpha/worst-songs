[Intro - spoken, solo piano]
Tuesday. 5:15 p.m.
Garden center. Entrance B.

[Verse 1]
There were two glass doors at the garden center
I pulled on the left like I always do
I turned around like a proper gentleman
And way past the lumber, there was you
Forty yards out with a cart full of mulch
One squeaky wheel and a toddler too
Then our eyes met across the parking lot
And that was it. I was holding it for you.

[Pre-Chorus]
So you started to jog - that apologetic jog
That "oh no, you really don't have to" jog
And I gave you the smile - the "take your time" smile
While that squeaky wheel squeaked for the better part of a mile

[Chorus]
I'm holding the door (holding the door)
Like a man who was born knowing what hands are for
You can stop at the pansies, I'm not keeping score
Oh, I'm holding the door
(Holding the door)

[Verse 2]
Then the little one dropped a sandal in the gravel
You went back for it, and I respected that
Forty-seven people walked through while I waited
One of them tipped me. One of them called me Pat.
The manager came out and handed me a vest
Said, "Son, I have never seen commitment like this"
And you were twenty yards out when I watched you stop
To consider a birdbath.
(A birdbath.)

[Pre-Chorus 2]
Then you started to jog - that apologetic jog
That "oh my gosh, I'm so sorry" jog
And I gave you the nod - the "no problem at all" nod
While my shoulder quietly prayed out loud to God

[Chorus]
I'm holding the door (holding the door)
Like a man who was born knowing what hands are for
You can stop at the pansies, I'm not keeping score
Oh, I'm holding the door
(Holding the door)

[Bridge]
Then the summer turned to autumn by the rakes and the hose
And the winter came and settled on my nose
Your baby learned to walk, and then learned to drive
And she honked as she passed me at a quarter after five

[Spoken, over rising strings]
People ask me, "Why didn't you just let it close?"
And I tell them:
A man who lets go at forty yards
will let go of anything.
They wrote a song about me. Worst Song Ever #20.
I haven't heard it. I've been busy.

[Final Chorus - key change, full choir]
I'm holding the door (holding the door)
In a vest that says PAT with a shoulder that's sore
Through the birdbath, the sandal, and forty-seven more
You can stop at the pansies, I'm not keeping score
Oh, I'm HOLDING THE DOOR
(Holding the door)

[Outro - solo piano, spoken]
And then... you were here.
Same cart. Same mulch. Kid's in college now.
You looked me in the eye.
You said, "Thank you so much."
And you walked through the door on the right.
...
It was automatic.

[Choir, one last chord]
Holding the door...
