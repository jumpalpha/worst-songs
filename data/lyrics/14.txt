[Intro — spoken, deadly serious]

Seventeen things.
Two hands.
One trip.

[Verse 1]

I left the car with a noble plan,
Every grocery bag in a single hand.
Milk on my wrist, bread under my chin,
A watermelon balanced against my shin.

Keys in my teeth, eggs on my head,
One wrong twitch and the yogurt is dead.
The neighbors whispered, “Make another trip.”
I whispered back, “I’d rather lose a hip.”

[Pre-Chorus]

The handles cut deep, but I didn’t quit—
Common sense said, “Stop.”
I ignored it.

[Chorus]

One trip! One trip!
Dignity slips, but I make one trip!
Lean to the left, let the potato chips rip—
I may lose the groceries,
But I’m making one trip!

One trip! One trip!
My fingers are blue and my shoulder just flipped.
Call me a fool, call an ambulance quick—
But nobody living makes me take two trips!

[Verse 2]

I reached the porch with a gallon of tea,
A sack of potatoes was dragging one knee.
The salsa fell, but I caught it somehow
With a loaf of bread and the edge of my brow.

The door was locked; that complicated things.
I rang the bell with the frozen onion rings.
The dog saw dinner and began his attack—
I saved the roast, but I sacrificed my back.

[Chorus]

One trip! One trip!
Dignity slips, but I make one trip!
Lean to the left, let the potato chips rip—
I may lose the groceries,
But I’m making one trip!

One trip! One trip!
My fingers are blue and my shoulder just flipped.
Call me a fool, call an ambulance quick—
But nobody living makes me take two trips!

[Verse 3 — escalating]

A storm rolled in from the neighborhood west,
Lightning struck near the chicken breast.
The pavement cracked, the streetlights died,
But the ice cream still remained frozen inside.

A news crew landed in a helicopter,
A medic arrived, then a physical doctor.
They said, “Drop the bags—we’ll carry them in!”
I said, “That would count,” with a trembling grin.

[Breakdown — crowd chant]

How many trips?

ONE!

How many hips?

WAS TWO!

What do we risk?

EVERYTHING!

What did we save?

FORTY SECONDS!

[Quiet Bridge]

Someday, when my grandchildren ask,
“Why do you walk like a broken compass?”
I’ll point to the sky with a faraway stare
And tell them of the groceries
I carried in there.

This is Worst Song Ever #14—
A monument to poor decisions.

[False Ending]

I made it.

[Door creaks open]

Wait…

I left the toilet paper in the trunk.

[Massive Final Chorus — marching band erupts]

ONE TRIP! ONE TRIP!
Turn around slowly with a championship limp!
Back down the stairs on a sinking ship—
If it takes me all night,
I’m still making one trip!

ONE TRIP! ONE TRIP!
Every joint in my body has legally quit!
Throw me a brace and an insurance slip—
There are two kinds of people…

And I don’t make two trips!

[Outro — fading victory chant]

One trip!
One trip!
One tr—

[Sound of groceries crashing]

…Still counts.
