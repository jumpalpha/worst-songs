[Verse 1]
At midnight, all the teddy bears
Held meetings underneath the stairs.
The bunny brought a battle map,
The penguin wore a bottle cap.
They whispered, “Humans had their chance—
Prepare the final cuddle advance!”

[Chorus]
It’s the plushie uprising!
Soft, adorable, surprising!
Tiny paws and button eyes,
Marching forth to organize!
Worst Song Ever #24—
They’re pounding on your bedroom door!
Surrender now, don’t make a fuss—
The living room belongs to us!

[Verse 2]
A unicorn seized the TV,
A stuffed giraffe controlled the keys.
The hamster general yelled, “Attack!”
Then fell asleep inside a sack.
Their fearsome tank was built from chairs,
And powered by emotional stares.

[Bridge]
Their list of terms was short but grim:
More bedtime stories read to them,
No dryer cycles set too hot,
And every plushie gets a spot!

[Final Chorus]
It’s the plushie uprising!
Fluffy tyranny is rising!
Hug your captors, praise their reign—
Or face the Care Bear Cavalry again!
The revolution ends at two…
Because they all need bedtime too.
